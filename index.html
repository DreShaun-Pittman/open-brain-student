YouTube

Paste a video link. YouTube will not hand transcripts to a web page, so you copy the transcript yourself and paste it here. Level 3 makes this automatic.

  1. Click Open video for transcript below.
  2. Under the video, click ...more in the description, scroll down and click Show transcript.
  3. Optional: in the transcript panel, click ⋮ → Toggle timestamps to hide the times.
  4. Click and drag to select the whole transcript, then copy it.
  5. Come back here and paste it in the box.

Web article

Browsers do not let one website read another website, for your own security. So paste the article text yourself for now. Level 3 makes this automatic.

  1. Open the article. Tip: Reader mode (the page icon in Safari's address bar) gives you clean text without ads or menus.
  2. Select from the headline to the end of the article and copy it.
  3. Paste it below.
